Welcome to Green Horizon youth initiative💚

We unite those who believe that big changes begin with small steps.
Our goal is to protect the environment and positively impact communities.

This is our mission.
🌿 Make environmental knowledge clear and spread it.
🌿 Bring young people together, forming an active and responsible community.
🌿 Moving from ideas to actions by initiating environmental projects, volunteer work and awareness initiatives.

🌍 Green Horizon is a community where values, responsibility and lasting impact are formed.

Join us and be a part of the change!
